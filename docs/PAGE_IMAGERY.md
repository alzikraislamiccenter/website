# Page imagery

These 34 illustrative images were generated with the built-in imagegen tool for the Al Zikra website. They depict fictional scenes. They are not photographs of Al Zikra's actual people, buildings or events. The optimized WebP files live in `public/assets/page-imagery/`.

The current visual direction shows only male learners, teachers, staff and family members. Eighteen previously mixed/female images were edited with imagegen to preserve their scene and composition while changing the people shown; this includes three Gallery images. The female stock clip was removed from the Gallery.
Updated page assets use a `-male.webp` filename so browsers request the replacements without showing a cached earlier image.

## Prompt set

All images used the `photorealistic-natural` use case: premium candid/editorial photography, realistic South Asian male subjects where people appear, modest dress, warm beige/walnut/muted-gold palette, natural light, no logos, watermarks, signage or legible text. Wide hero prompts requested a 16:9 composition with the main subject on the right and dark uncluttered space on the left for white page headings. About section images requested portrait 4:5 composition. Blog covers requested landscape 16:10 composition. The edit prompts asked imagegen to keep those compositions and replace every female person with a male person of comparable age.
